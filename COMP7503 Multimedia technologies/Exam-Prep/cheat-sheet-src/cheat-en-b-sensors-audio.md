### Sensors, touch, errors, and waves
**Sensor:** a device that measures a physical quantity and converts it into a signal that a human or a machine can use. Pipeline: physical quantity -> transduction -> conditioning/amplification -> ADC -> analysis. Sound and light sensors matter most for multimedia. e.g. a thermometer turns temperature into a voltage then a number.
**Sensor categories (传感器类别):** (1) imitate human senses; (2) measure aspects of life; (3) measure physical facts about the environment.
**Touch:** skin = largest organ; tactile sensors translate pressure into signals (pressure, resistive, capacitive). Resistive (电阻式触控): pressing makes two conductive layers contact; robust to dust/water, needs a firm press (supermarket/7-Eleven self-checkout). Capacitive (电容式触控): finger changes capacitance; light touch, multi-touch; water/insulating gloves disturb it. 3-D Touch measures force. Actuator (执行器) gives feedback (vibration/Taptic Engine): input sensing differs from output feedback.
**Sight:** CCD/CMOS sensing elements (photovoltaic principle); more elements -> higher resolution. Brain (spot-the-difference): recognise objects -> occipital (枕叶); spatial relations -> occipital + parietal (顶叶); short-term memory -> frontal (额叶) + parietal; marking/movement -> frontal. Binocular disparity (双目视差)/stereograms give depth.
**Hearing:** audio sensors convert changes in air pressure into electrical signals (dynamic and condenser microphones).
**Smell/taste:** chemistry not well understood; no proper artificial nose/tongue yet. Other species sense temperature, gravity, magnetic fields, humidity, infrared.
**Life sensors:** weight (+ height/gender/age -> BMI = kg/m^2), body temperature, noise (loud environments, construction sites), heart rate, ECG (sinus rhythm), movement (+ heart rate -> calories, a model estimate). Care-on-Call watch for seniors -> call centre -> ambulance.
**Environment sensors:** Accelerometer (加速度计) (orientation, pedometer), gyroscope (陀螺仪) (rotation), GPS (location), radiation sensor, altimeter (高度计) (altitude). More reliable than human perception (spatial disorientation; you cannot "feel" 100 km/h); cars carry 50+ sensors.
**Sensor limitations:** (1) limited dynamic range (动态范围): only inputs within an interval (shadows vs highlights lost); fix: HDR or sensor arrays covering sub-ranges (0-120 + 120-240); (2) offset (偏置)/bias: nonzero output at zero input (y=kx+b), calibrate (校准); (3) nonlinearity (非线性): linear input change gives non-linear output; tune to be linear in an operating range; (4) hysteresis (迟滞): "sensor response deviation over time"; curve differs rising vs falling (5->10 gives 2->5, falling takes another path, so output 3 maps to two inputs).
**Sound:** a travelling longitudinal pressure wave (纵向压力波), an oscillation of pressure through solid, liquid or gas; cannot travel through vacuum. e.g. a shout in air (no sound in space). Air 343 m/s (= Mach 1, 20 C), water 1482 m/s; lambda=v/f. Hearing: slides give 12 Hz-22 kHz and 20 Hz-22 kHz.
**SPL:** Lp (声压级)=20log10(p/pref)=10log10(p^2/pref^2) dB; p = RMS (均方根) pressure (sine: p_rms=p_peak/sqrt2); pref = 20 microPa air (threshold of hearing, a mosquito 3 m away), 1 microPa water. 0.2 Pa -> 80 dB; pressure ratio = 10^(dL/20); double pressure +6.02 dB, double power +3.01 dB. 0 dB is reference, not silence; dB is relative; do not normally add dB values. The ear is non-linear and less sensitive at low frequencies.
**A-weighting (A计权):** frequency weighting to match perceived loudness (ear less sensitive to low frequencies): add a table of values per octave/third-octave band, L_A(f)=L_p(f)+A(f) -> dBA. Same SPL at different frequencies need not sound equally loud.

### Acoustics, microphones, recording, and loudspeakers
**Echo (回声):** a reflection of sound perceived some time after the direct sound (water well, mountains); humans cannot separate it if delay < 0.1 s, but machines may still "hear" multiple sources. Distance d=vt/2 (0.1 s -> 17.15 m).
**Reverberation (混响):** in an enclosed space numerous echoes build up and slowly decay as the room absorbs the sound; hurts content analysis even when inaudible.
**Interference (干涉):** superposition of two or more waves giving a new wave pattern: same f and A in phase -> 2A; 180 deg out of phase -> cancel (basis of ANC; imperfect match leaves residual).
**Ear chain:** pinna/canal -> eardrum/ossicles -> cochlear (耳蜗) hair cells -> auditory nerve.
**Localisation:** ITD (双耳时间差) = arrival-time difference; ILD (双耳声级差) = level difference (both -> left/right); HRTF (头部相关传递函数) = volume/quality changes from pinna reflections (front/back/up/down). （声级差和时间差判断左右，耳廓滤波帮助辨别前后上下。）
**Microphone:** an acoustic sensor that converts sound into an electrical signal: a membrane exposed to sound pressure varies an electrical property (resistance, voltage, capacitance). e.g. a phone mic captures your voice for a call.
**Dynamic microphone (动圈麦克风):** diaphragm moves a coil in a permanent magnet's field -> induced voltage; no power supply needed. Condenser (电容麦克风): diaphragm/backplate distance changes C=epsilon*A/d; needs bias power; more sensitive.
**Directionality / polar response (指向性):** sensitivity to pressure waves arriving at different angles; polar pattern = locations giving the same output for a constant SPL source; varies with frequency. Omni (全指向): all angles, good in quiet studios, bad at rejecting noise; cardioid (心形): max rejection 180 deg; supercardioid (超心形): narrower front, rear lobe, max rejection 120 deg; shotgun (枪式): highly directional; bidirectional (双指向/8字形): front/rear.
**Frequency response (频率响应):** output ideally proportional to pressure; linear only for certain SPL and frequency ranges.
**Gain (增益):** analog output -> amplifier -> ADC. Too high: overdriven, clips (削波), distortion (unrepairable); too low: small values zero out after A/D. Keep headroom. Unity gain = x1 = 0 dB.
**AES42 digital output:** no analog interference problem; configurable polar pattern, low-cut (低切) 40/80/120 Hz (= high-pass filter (高通滤波器), removes rumble), gain unity to +63 dB, mute.
**Early recording:** sound pressure engraved on tinfoil around a rotating cylinder; a needle plays it back, mechanically amplified. An 18-note music box encodes notes, not arbitrary waveforms.
**Loudspeaker:** the most common sound-reproduction device; reverse of a microphone （麦克风：声→电；扬声器：电→声。）: current vibrates a diaphragm -> pressure waves in air/water; directional too.
**Speaker specs:** rated power (额定功率) = maximum input before damage (not loudness); impedance (阻抗) = electrical compatibility with amplifiers; crossover frequency (分频频率) = signal division by drivers; frequency range = linear response range.
**Drivers:** low frequencies are easier with a bigger diaphragm (larger mass vibrates slower), highs with small/light ones. Crossover: low-pass (低通) -> woofer (低音单元), high-pass -> tweeter (高音单元), band-pass -> midrange (中音单元).
**Enclosure:** Cabinet (箱体) prevents rear-diaphragm waves from cancelling front waves （振膜前后声波反相，直接相遇会削弱低音。）; passive radiator (无源辐射器) is moved by internal pressure, no voice coil.
**Surround:** 5.1 = front L/R, centre, surround L/R + LFE (低频效果声道); 6.1 adds rear centre; 7.1 adds rear L/R. Hard to deploy (space, wiring), so virtual surround (虚拟环绕声) (2.1, or a single strip = digital sound projection / sound-projector array (声音投射阵列)) recreates ILD, ITD, HRTF; depends on seat and room.

### Speech and music
**Speech ranges:** normal speech 80 Hz-5 kHz; pitch male 120-160 Hz, women/children 220-330 Hz (children/women high, old men low); vowels up to 5 kHz; sibilants (咝音) (s, f) up to 20 kHz; 60 dBA normal speech at 60 cm, yelling 76 dBA male / 68 dBA female. Airflow excites vocal folds (声带); F0 = pitch; vocal-tract resonances form formants (共振峰).
**Speech sounds (phonemes):** Stops (塞音): blocked airflow (/p,t,k/); fricatives (擦音): partially blocked, turbulent (/f,v/); approximants (近音): slight turbulence (/w,r/); aspirate (送气音): exhaled breath (/h/); vowels (元音): full unimpeded airflow. Consonants + vowels = phonemes; vary by language.
**Spectrogram signatures:** time horizontal, frequency vertical, energy colour. Voiced (浊音): formant bands (yellow); unvoiced: no formants; stops: strong burst across all bands after a short silence; fricatives/aspirates: "large clouds" of smooth energy.
**ASR limitations:** people raise volume/change pitch/duration in noise, so quiet-trained models fail; phones sample at 8 kHz or below (Nyquist 4 kHz) so sibilants are lost; solution: spell with whole words ("Alpha, Beta, Charlie ... Sugar, Zero").
**TTS (文字转语音) and dictation:** text -> speech (Nuance demo; Hatsune Miku = synthesized singing); dictation -> NLP intent/entities -> action; perfect transcription does not ensure correct action.
**Pitch (音高):** a sound's perceived fundamental frequency (f0/F0). Harmonic (谐波): k*f0; partial (分音): any component (overtones need not be integer multiples); fundamental (基音) = 1st harmonic; 1st overtone (泛音) = 2nd harmonic. 440/880/1320/1760 Hz = 1st-4th harmonics = fundamental/1st/2nd/3rd overtone.
**Timbre:** the quality that distinguishes different types of sound (e.g. piano vs violin on the same note); characterised by the frequency spectrum (the sum of distinct frequencies emitted for a note) and the time envelope (包络). Teacher's piano/violin question: same middle C (about 262 Hz) fundamental, different overtone mix -> different timbre.
**ADSR (起音/衰减/持续/释音):** Attack rise, Decay to Sustain, Sustain held level, Release after excitation stops.
**Instruments:** strings vibrate (excitation sets the harmonic mix); wind blows into a resonator; percussion is hit, shaken, rubbed, scraped. Music-analysis goals: compress audio, detect instruments, manipulate/synthesise music.
**Synthesis:** physical modelling (harmonics + envelope/ADSR) or original samples (wavetables (波表)): generate wave shapes, several tones for fundamental + harmonics, vary volume over time. Storing F0 alone cannot recreate timbre (LEGO idea: build complex sound from simple sinusoids).
