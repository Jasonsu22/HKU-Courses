### Audio analysis, coding, and communication
**ANC (主动降噪):** a microphone measures noise, the circuit makes an opposite (180 deg) waveform, the speaker plays it: n + (-n) approximately 0 (destructive interference). Same idea in cars/buses: 3 ceiling mics + ECU + engine speed -> speakers play anti-noise. Limited by latency, phase/amplitude mismatch, position, changing sound.
**Call noise reduction (通话降噪):** a near-mouth main mic (speech + noise) plus reference mic(s) (noise) -> subtract/suppress; different from cancelling the ear sound field. More mics exploit spatial differences but cost size, power, headroom (峰值余量) (iPhone 4: 2 mics; 5s/6: 3; 6s: 4; Amazon Echo: 7). Constraints: distant speech, background noise, always-on power, overload, size.
**EQ (均衡器):** fine-tunes gain/attenuation of frequency bands across 20 Hz-20 kHz; presets (Bass Booster, Classical, Jazz, Rock) are gain curves; >200 bands in high-end systems; +/-6 to +/-24 dB. It changes timbre (音色); volume changes overall level. Cannot separate speech and noise in the same band; excessive boost distorts.
**ASR (自动语音识别):** waveform -> A/D -> denoise/normalize -> short segments (hundredths/thousandths of a second) -> match phoneme (音素) (~40 English phonemes) -> statistical/language model -> words. Accent, noise, homophones, mismatched conditions cause errors; ASR is not speaker recognition or translation.
**Shazam:** audio -> spectrogram (声谱图) -> salient peaks = fingerprint (音频指纹) of each catalogue song, indexed by song ID/time. A 10-s query gives the same landmarks; matching peaks vote for a common time offset (时间偏移) -> song + position, ranked by similarity score. Slide: robust to noise, room reverb, EQ, overlapping sounds (stable peaks survive), though not every distortion.
**LPC (线性预测编码) vocoder:** vocoder (声码器) = voice + coder; based on the human vocal model: air through the epiglottis vibrates vocal cords (voiced) and/or resonates in the mouth/nose cavity. Source (periodic pulses = voiced, noise = unvoiced) -> vocal-tract filter (声道滤波器). Send filter coefficients, energy, pitch, voiced/unvoiced; GSM voice is LPC-based; other use: speech synthesis / voice transformation. Models speech production, not a general music model.
**Ear (for MPEG):** eardrum vibration -> hydraulic pressure in the fluid-filled cochlea -> 20,000+ hair cells (high tones stimulate short hairs; base = high, apex = low frequency, like piano keys) -> the ear senses a spectrogram.
**MPEG audio:** lossy coding (有损编码) based on psychoacoustics (心理声学). Frequency masking (频率掩蔽): a loud sound in one band hides a softer sound in another band that would be audible without it. Temporal masking (时间掩蔽): after a loud sound stops, a quiet sound is inaudible for a short time (the ear turns its gain down and takes time to turn it up). Absolute threshold varies with frequency (most sensitive mid-kHz); a component below the raised masking curve is inaudible.
**MP3:** Layer I basic (DCC digital tapes), II advanced bit allocation, III adds hybrid filters; MP3 = MPEG Audio Layer III, not MPEG-3. 1152 samples (about 26 ms at 44.1 kHz) -> 32 bands -> masked frequencies via psychoacoustic model -> bit allocation/quantization (the lossy step) -> variable-length coding: entropy/VLC coding (熵编码/变长编码) removes redundancy without extra loss.

### Media applications: imaging, video, vision, retrieval
**HDR (高动态范围):** fuses aligned complementary exposures: long exposure keeps shadows, short keeps highlights. "Dynamic" = brightness range, not motion. Needs registration; moving objects ghost.
**Night Mode (夜景模式) / Deep Fusion:** frames at various shutter speeds combined to recover shadow detail and avoid motion blur; Deep Fusion (iPhone 11 Pro, 2019) = 4 short + 1 long + 4 secondary = 9 frames, optimised for detail and low noise.
**Super-resolution (超分辨率):** shifted multi-frame samples improve spatial detail (also satellite images: "if one shot is not enough, take more").
**Retinex (视网膜皮层增强):** I=R*L; estimates/reduces illumination influence to enhance reflectance visibility/contrast; over-processing adds noise/halos.
**Stabilization (防抖/稳像):** stabilises shaky footage: track background feature points across frames -> estimate global camera motion -> compensate/smooth trajectory (轨迹); may crop edges; fails on moving foregrounds; does not remove within-exposure blur (drones, ships, phones do it in-chip).
**Motion blur (运动模糊):** motion accumulated during exposure, from global camera motion or fast objects; different parts of one object can have different velocities, so no single global motion model. FPS is not exposure time; higher ISO/gain does not collect more photons.
**Deblurring (去模糊) pipeline:** blurred video -> foreground mask -> segmentation/corners -> feature tracking (HBMA) -> motion estimate -> blur kernel (模糊核) -> deconvolution (反卷积)/restoration. Wrong kernels, noise and lost information prevent perfect recovery.
**Vision tasks:** detection (检测) = whether/where; recognition (识别) = identity; tracking (跟踪) = identity across frames; reconstruction (重建) = hidden/3-D structure; retrieval (检索) = ranks indexed material. A detection box is not identity.
**Face detection (feature-based, Viola-Jones):** Haar features (哈尔特征) = neighbouring light/dark rectangle-sum differences, computed efficiently; AdaBoost (自适应提升) combines weak classifiers into a strong one; a cascade (级联) rejects non-faces early; scan every location and scale.
**THREE GENERATIONS of face recognition (teacher stressed):** (1) feature/distance-based: eye-eye, eye-nose, nose-mouth distances as a "digital ID"; about 30-40% accuracy, fails for twins and changing expressions; (2) model-based: fit a general appearance model/mesh (<100 parameters; "Mission Impossible mask"), parameters = features, tolerates expression/angle; (3) deep learning: millions of weights learn features automatically, >80-90%, used at HK immigration and bank apps.
**Head tracking (model-based):** fit a q1-q8 spline head contour; a tripwire (虚拟绊线) counts people only when trajectories cross a virtual line, capturing the head there. False positives: faces on T-shirts, backpacks; occlusion and broken tracks cause errors.
**3-D face reconstruction (三维人脸重建):** recognition works well only on frontal faces, so convert non-frontal -> frontal: (1) feature detection: fit frontal/half-profile/profile AAMs, keep the minimum residual error; (2) 3-D modelling: personalise a generic mesh (网格) (6292 vertices, 6152 facets); texture map (纹理贴图) holds face texture, texture coordinates map 2-D texture onto the 3-D mesh; (3) pose estimation (姿态估计): rotation/location/scale; (4) texture rebuilding: fill/interpolate, postprocess, render frontal. Similarity scores rise, e.g. 0.33 -> 0.55, 0.00 -> 0.85. Hidden texture is inferred; reduces pose mismatch, not all errors.
**Recognition limits:** CMU: specially designed glasses frames fool recognition or impersonate someone; York: even ordinary glasses hurt HUMAN recognition of unfamiliar faces. Face ID adds structured IR/depth cues, not absolute anti-spoofing.
**Gait (步态):** "one of the most important biometrics"; identifies people at low resolution (few pixels); "KGB walk" (one arm still); Mission Impossible: face -> iris -> gait. Silhouette (轮廓) -> match a 16-pose codebook (码本) (dictionary idea, one-hot codeword per frame) -> Bag-of-Gait vector -> compare. Clothing, carried objects, speed, viewpoint matter.
**Video retrieval:** H.264/MPEG-4 stream -> motion vectors + background model -> moving-object segmentation -> tracking -> per-object features (size, colour histogram bins, edge-orientation bins 0/45/90/135 deg, trajectory) -> metadata index DB -> photo/sketch query -> ranked clips. Pyramid: video data -> metadata -> analytics -> insights. Teacher: the hard part is the index, not the search (library book ID; Google PageRank); similar colours do not prove the same object.

### XR, DIKW, and city systems
**VR (虚拟现实) / AR (增强现实):** VR presents a virtual environment; AR overlays digital content on the real view; both need viewpoint tracking and low-latency rendering. Metaverse interview (Lex Fridman/Zuckerberg): static 3-D avatar + real-time expression/head parameters.
**DIKW (数据-信息-知识-智慧) (slide):** Data (acquire/tag/cleanse/correlate) -> Information (context; reports/dashboards) -> Knowledge (patterns; predictive analytics (预测性分析)) -> Wisdom (action; prescriptive analytics (决策建议型分析)). Uses: forecasting, recommendation, anomaly/fraud detection, maintenance. Example: recordings -> clean/ASR/labels -> grouped complaints -> hypothesized product issue -> repair decision; correlation alone is not causation.
**Smart city (智慧城市):** distributed sound-leak, gas, wind, bridge-vibration, video sensors -> network -> domain cleaning/fusion -> anomaly/prediction -> warning/control. A phone is also sensing -> analysis -> action. Teacher: understand the methodology/flow of each application, not implementation details.
