### Light, photometry, imaging, and depth
**Light:** an EM wave (电磁波) with wavelength 400-780 nm; c=299,792,458 about 3*10^8 m/s, c=f*lambda. Teacher: amplitude = brightness/intensity (intensity proportional to E-field amplitude squared); frequency/wavelength = colour (white = mixture of all frequencies); phase = coherence.
**Coherence:** Monochromatic (单色光) = one frequency; coherent (相干) = stable relative phase (laser); light from normal sources is not in phase (non-coherent).
**Polarization (偏振):** describes the light wave's orientation; uncorrelated field orientations = unpolarized; filters pass one orientation. e.g. polarised sunglasses cut glare; 3-D cinema glasses. Anaglyph (红蓝立体) colour-filter stereo differs from polarization stereo.
**Reflection (反射):** incident ray, reflected ray and normal lie in one plane; angle of reflection = angle of incidence. Specular (镜面反射) = directional; diffuse reflection (漫反射) spreads.
**Refraction (折射):** the "bending" of light rays from one transparent material to another, because wavelength changes while frequency stays; n=c/v, n1 sin(theta1)=n2 sin(theta2); forms images in lenses/eyes. e.g. a straw looks bent in water. Mirrors and transparent objects complicate computer-vision detection.
**Photometry (光度学):** weights radiation by the luminosity function (光视效率函数) (average spectral sensitivity of human vision; photopic (明视觉) and scotopic curves differ); radiometry (辐射度学) uses watts, so electrical W is not lm.
**Three units:** candela cd = luminous intensity (发光强度), power emitted in a particular direction; lumen lm = luminous flux (光通量), the total light emitted; lux lx = illuminance (照度), luminous power per area. 1 lm = 1 cd*sr; isotropic 1 cd -> 4pi lm; 1 lx = 1 lm/m^2 (1000 lm on 1 m^2 = 1000 lx; on 10 m^2 = 100 lx). （发光强度看方向，光通量看总输出，照度看单位面积接收多少光。）
**Which unit when (teacher):** buying a bulb/projector -> lm (total = sum over all directions); light toward one direction/receiver -> cd; desk/room lighting and camera low-light rating (minimum lux) -> lx. 450 lm = 40 W incandescent = 9-11 W CFL = 6-8 W LED; 100 W bulb about 120 cd. Office 320-500 lx, TV studio 1000 lx, desert sun up to 130,000 lx, full moon about 1 lx; eyes can detect a single photon.
**Solid angle (立体角):** radian cuts an arc equal to r; steradian cuts an area r^2; Omega = A/r^2, full sphere 4pi sr. Example: 50 mm*50 mm receiver at 2 m receives 0.1 W: A=.0025 m^2, Omega=.000625 sr, I=160 W/sr = radiant intensity (辐射强度), not cd. 1 cd = 540*10^12 Hz light at 1/683 W/sr.
**Image recording:** camera obscura ("dark chamber") / pinhole camera (针孔相机) projects but cannot store; first light-sensitive memory = paper with silver chloride; then film; now photosensitive electronics (light -> charge -> ADC).
**Image formation:** geometry (where a scene point lands on the image plane) + physics (brightness from illumination and surface properties).
**Perspective:** virtual upright image plane (虚拟正立像平面) x=fX/Z, y=fY/Z; size proportional to f/Z (larger f bigger, farther smaller); rear physical plane flips signs. Points on one ray share a projection, so one view loses depth. （焦距增大则像变大，距离增大则像变小。） Aperture (光圈) controls light/depth of field (景深); long exposure risks blur.
**Resolution / cameras:** one photoelectric sensor = one pixel; MP=W*H/10^6; more pixels do not guarantee detail. Photo camera = still images (slide: up to 32 MP); video camera = sequence (FPS), compressed into one file (up to 16 MP). 720p=1280*720, 1080p=1920*1080, UHD 4K=3840*2160. Aspect ratio 4:3 (VGA 640*480, VCD/analog TV 720*576) -> 16:9 because two eyes give a wide field of view.
**Stereo:** separated viewpoints -> disparity (视差) d -> Z=fB/d (small disparity = far); phones carry 2-3 cameras like two eyes (close one eye, depth lost); multiple cameras give focal-length/FOV range and fusion, not just more megapixels.
**ToF (飞行时间):** active; emits modulated near-IR (850/940 nm), measures return delay/phase -> depth: Z=c*Delta t/2 (20 ns -> 3 m); multipath/ambient light limits.
**Folded optics (折叠光路) / Leap Motion:** prism folds a long lens path into a thin phone; Leap Motion uses IR cameras for hand pose (range/occlusion limits).

### Displays, vision, color, and final distinctions
**Subtractive (减色法):** relies on reflected ambient light, absorbing parts of it; needs light. e.g. printed ink/toner on paper. CMYK; K not redundant (CMY black is muddy brown, 3x ink, wets paper, blurs text).
**Additive (加色法):** active light sources mixed together. e.g. a TV/monitor. RGB: zero = black, full mix = white. A red chair absorbs other colours and reflects red. Gamut (色域) = reproducible colour range; RGB->CMYK can lose colours.
**Displays:** CRT: electron gun beam excites phosphor; LCD: backlight modulated by liquid crystal (液晶)/polarizers/colour filters (TN twists; IPS in-plane, wider angle); OLED: pixels emit, no backlight, true black; AMOLED = active-matrix OLED. Early Kindle e-ink is reflective (no backlight).
**Glasses-free 3-D:** parallax barrier (视差屏障) sends different views to each eye; must sit in sweet spots or images blur. Gatebox "3D hologram" is a virtual-character display, not true holography.
**Eye:** iris (虹膜) controls light entering; lens focuses on the retina (视网膜) (cones and rods) -> chemical/nerve signals -> brain via optic nerve.
**Photopic vision (bright):** Cones (视锥细胞), 6-7 million, mainly in the fovea (中央凹), each with its own nerve end; colour + fine detail.
**Scotopic vision (dim):** rods (视杆细胞), 75-150 million, spread over the retina, several per nerve; general overall picture, no colour. Optic disc (视盘/盲点) has no receptors.
**Resolving power:** fovea about 1.5 mm x 1.5 mm x 150,000 cones/mm^2 = 337,500 (a medium CCD fits that in <= 5 mm x 5 mm); 1080p = 2,073,600 px (about 6x) but the fovea covers about 2 degrees. Eyes never stay still (Yarbus): the brain builds a higher-resolution image from snapshots; saccade (扫视) + fixation (注视); foveated rendering (注视点渲染) needs fast eye tracking (VR).
**Subjective brightness (主观亮度):** intensity as perceived by the visual system; a logarithmic function of incident light; context-dependent. e.g. the same grey looks brighter on a dark background. Brightness adaptation: about 10^10 overall, not simultaneously.
**Colour spaces:** CMYK for printing; RGB(A) for display (alpha = opacity); YUV for compression: luma (亮度) Y' and chroma (色度) U/V (eye less sensitive to colour -> chroma subsampling 4:2:0; B/W TV compatible; DVD component cables Y, Pb, Pr).
**YUV formulas:** Y'=.299R+.587G+.114B; U=-.14713R-.28886G+.436B; V=.615R-.51499G-.10001B; R=Y'+1.13983V; G=Y'-.39465U-.58060V; B=Y'+2.03211U. R=G=B -> U,V about 0. Y' is weighted, not mean(R,G,B); YCbCr conventions vary.
**True Tone (原彩显示):** senses ambient light and shifts the display's white so whites look the same under any lighting; not higher resolution or HDR.
